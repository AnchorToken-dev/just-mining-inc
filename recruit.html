Quick FAQ

I don't have much hash power. Can I still join?

Absolutely. There's no minimum TH — if you're active and show up for wars, you're welcome. Small miners who participate beat big miners who vanish.

How are rewards split?

Rewards are distributed based on each member's contribution to the clan's points. The harder you mine, the more you earn — but everyone gets a fair cut.

What happens if I go inactive?

Members who miss multiple war cycles get removed to make room for active miners. It keeps the clan honest.
