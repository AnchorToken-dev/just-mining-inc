How Wars Work

Miner Wars rounds are tied to real Bitcoin blocks. When a round starts, our clan competes against another for points. Points come from a public formula based on our members' hash power, energy efficiency, and spell usage.

  • Rounds — run on the Bitcoin block schedule
  • Cycles — leagues reshuffle every cycle based on performance
  • Spells — coordinated by officers in our chat for maximum impact
  • Rewards — real BTC, split by contribution
