About Us

The short version: we mine Bitcoin. The long version is still pretty short.

What Is Miner Wars?

Miner Wars is GoMining's PvP game. Players pool their hash power into clans, compete in rounds tied to real Bitcoin blocks, earn points from a public formula, and win real BTC rewards. Clans move between leagues every cycle based on performance alone — no favoritism, no exceptions.

Who We Are

Just Mining, Inc. started with a simple frustration: too many clans are full of inactive members, whale-dominated reward splits, and drama. We wanted a clan where:

  • Active members matter — we purge inactive miners regularly
  • Rewards are fair — no one whale hoarding the payout
  • Strategy is real — coordinated spell usage in war rounds
  • Communication is easy — active Discord/Telegram, quick answers

Our rule is simple: show up, mine, and split the rewards. If you're consistent, you'll fit right in. If you disappear for weeks, don't be surprised when your spot goes to someone who's actually digging.

What We're Not

We're not a whale sanctuary. We're not a place to park your miner and vanish. We're not going to promise you a Lambo. We're just a group of people who mine together and split the winnings fairly. No drama. Just mining.
