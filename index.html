WHO WE ARE

Miner Wars is GoMining's PvP game. Players pool their hash power into clans, compete in rounds tied to real Bitcoin blocks, earn points from a public formula, and win real BTC rewards.

Just Mining, Inc. started with a simple idea: a clan where anyone can join, set their miners, and feel at home — no matter their hash power or how chatty they are. Our rule is simple: mine, split rewards fairly, and don't be a jerk. We're not a whale sanctuary and we're not a hype machine. We're just a group of people who mine together.

⚔️ Server Battles

Compete across the Dune server every cycle. Mine hard, earn points, take your share of the BTC.

📊 Fair Splits

No huge whales soaking up all the rewards. Every active member's contribution counts.

👥 Everyone Welcome

Mine at your own pace. Chat if you want, lurk if you don't. Big miners and small miners alike.

🧠 Spell Strategy

Coordinated spell usage in battles — we play smart, not just loud.
